## Production notes

- Vertical 9:16, captions burned in, no voice-over required (Spotlight is watched muted
  first).
- Use the console's dark UI as the backdrop for code zooms; monospace font, one highlighted
  line per shot.
- The payoff shot is the chain sealing step by step — hold it a beat longer than the rest.
- Never show real evidence or hostnames in the footage; use the demo run.
